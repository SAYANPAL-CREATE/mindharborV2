Suggestions for your wellbeing

How to read your scores

Depression: PHQ-9, score out of 27. Anxiety: GAD-7, score out of 21. Stress: exploratory four-question check, score out of 12 (not clinically validated). Percentages show points earned divided by the maximum possible points, not the probability of having a condition. Your results should be discussed with a qualified clinician if you have concerns.

Sources: PHQ-9 & GAD-7 scoring guidance · Tele-MANAS

YOU'RE IN CONTROL

Your space. Your data.

Your wellbeing is personal. In V3, live AI is optional; written reflections are transmitted to a model provider only after you opt in.

Privacy by design, with limits.

The scoring stays in your browser. By default, the adaptive interview uses local rules. If you explicitly enable AI, your reflections and limited question history are sent via our server to the configured AI provider for analysis. The app does not intentionally store transcripts in a database or log message bodies. Your hosting and AI providers may process or retain data according to their policies; do not enter identifying patient information.

Remember completed reports on this device

Optional. Stores your last report locally in this browser without encryption. Avoid on shared computers.

PLEASE KNOW

Built for transparency

This code includes no analytics, advertising trackers, or account system.
AI is optional and uses a server-side API key. The backend selects only from the authored question bank. Without AI consent it uses local rules.
No login or profile required.
Local storage is unencrypted and can be accessed by others using the same browser profile.
Clearing browser data, private mode, or switching devices may erase saved information.
